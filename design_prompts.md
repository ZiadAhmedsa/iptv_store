## 🎨 1. The Design System: "Luxury Minimalist"
**Objective:** Create a high-end, agency-grade UI using modern design principles.

### A. The Core Concept (AI Prompt)
> "Design a User Interface (UI) based on the **Luxury Minimalist** style with **Glassmorphism** accents. The interface must feel premium (Executive Grade) with ultra-rounded corners (`2.5rem`+), deep semantic colors, and sophisticated gradients. Prioritize visual hierarchy and negative space (breathing room)."

### B. Visual Specifications
*   **Background:** Deep Slate (`#0f172a`).
*   **Surface/Cards:** Slate-900 with transparent white borders (`border-white/5`).
*   **Accents:** Brand Gradient from Indigo-500 (`#6366f1`) to Purple-600 (`#9333ea`).
*   **Typography:** Cairo Font (Google Fonts).
    *   **Titles:** Black (900 weight) + `tracking-tighter`.
    *   **Body:** Bold (700) or Medium (500).
*   **Border Radius:** `rounded-[2.5rem]` for main cards, `rounded-2xl` for inputs/buttons.
*   **Effects:** `backdrop-blur-xl`, `shadow-2xl`, and subtle `animate-pulse` on decorative glows.

### C. Reference Tailwind CSS Classes
*   **Premium Card:** `bg-white/80 dark:bg-slate-900/50 backdrop-blur-2xl rounded-[3rem] border border-slate-200 dark:border-white/5 shadow-2xl`
*   **Call to Action:** `brand-gradient text-white px-10 py-4 rounded-2xl font-black text-lg transition-all transform hover:-translate-y-1`
*   **Hero Title:** `text-5xl md:text-8xl font-black tracking-tighter leading-tight`

## 🔔 2. The Alerts System: "Premium Response"
**Objective:** Maintain the luxury feel even during system feedback.

### A. The Alert Concept (AI Prompt)
> "Configure a SweetAlert2 notification system that follows a **Dark Mode Luxury** theme. The alert must use high-end typography (Cairo), premium rounded corners, and brand-consistent colors for buttons and icons. It should feel like an integral part of a high-end dashboard, not a third-party plugin."

### B. Technical Details
*   **Dark Mode BG:** `#0f172a`.
*   **Confirm Button:** `#6366f1` (Brand Indigo).
*   **Radius:** `rounded-[2rem]`.
*   **Animation:** Use a smooth fade-in instead of a bounce.

## 🚀 Pro-Tips for Implementation
1.  **Breathing Room:** Always use generous padding (`p-10` or `p-12`) for cards to maintain the "Luxury" feel.
2.  **Subtle Borders:** Never use solid black or gray borders. Use `white/5` (white with 5% opacity) to create "light edges" that look modern.
3.  **Typography Weight:** Use the `Black 900` weight of the Cairo font for Arabic titles to make them look "Designed" rather than just "Typed".
